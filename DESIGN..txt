## Brand & Style
The design system focuses on clarity, weather-responsive immersion, and the structured utility of Material Design 3. The target audience includes daily commuters, travelers, and outdoor enthusiasts who require immediate, glanceable data.

The style is a refined **Modern Corporate** approach infused with **Glassmorphism**. It utilizes the "Surface Container" logic of Material 3 but enhances it with translucent overlays to allow weather-state backgrounds (dynamic gradients or imagery) to bleed through the UI. The emotional response should be calm, reliable, and highly legible, even in high-glare outdoor conditions.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in a Primary Sky Blue, serving as the functional anchor for interactions. Slate Gray provides a grounded secondary tone for metadata and inactive states. Sunlit Gold is reserved for highlight events (clear skies, UV warnings), while Rain Indigo signifies precipitation and evening forecasts.

This design system utilizes **dynamic tonal palettes**. In the dark mode, surfaces shift to deep charcoals with high-translucency overlays to maintain depth. Semantic colors for "Rain" (Indigo) and "Sun" (Gold) must maintain a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against their respective surface containers.

## Typography
The system uses **Roboto Flex** for its exceptional adaptability. 
- **Display Large** is reserved exclusively for the current temperature, using a light weight to feel modern and airy.
- **Headline** styles are used for location names and primary card headers.
- **Title** styles provide structure to internal card metrics (Wind, Humidity).
- **Label** styles are utilized for small-caps metadata and chart axes.

On mobile devices, the temperature display scales down to `display-lg-mobile` to ensure it remains centered within the viewport without breaking the visual hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing
This design system follows a **12-column fluid grid** for tablet and desktop, collapsing to a **4-column grid** for mobile. 

A strict 4px baseline grid is used. Information density should be managed through "Stack" units:
- **8px (sm):** Related metrics within a single group (e.g., Wind speed and direction icon).
- **16px (md):** Distance between independent cards or list items.
- **24px (lg):** Major section breaks, such as between the Hourly forecast and Daily outlook.

Margins should expand as the screen size increases to prevent data lines from becoming too long and unreadable.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Glassmorphism**.
- **Level 0 (Background):** Solid color or weather-state gradient.
- **Level 1 (Main Cards):** Surface color at 80% opacity with a 20px backdrop blur. No shadows; depth is implied by the blur of the background.
- **Level 2 (Overlays/Modals):** Surface color at 95% opacity with a subtle 1px inner border (Primary Fixed Dim) to define edges.
- **Interaction:** When pressed, cards do not lift (no shadows); instead, they apply a state layer (8% opacity overlay) of the `onSurface` color.

## Shapes
The design system adopts an **Extra-Large** corner radius strategy to feel friendly and organic, mimicking the fluidity of nature.
- **Containers/Cards:** Use `rounded-xl` (24px - 32px) to enclose forecast data.
- **Buttons/Search Bars:** Use full pill-shaped rounding (32px or height/2) to align with Android M3 standards.
- **Selection Indicators:** (like the active state in Segmented Controls) should use a 16px radius.

## Components
### M3 SearchBar
Positioned at the top of the main view. It should be a docked search bar with `surface-container-high` coloring, a leading search icon, and a trailing profile or settings icon.

### Segmented Controls
Used for toggling between "Today", "Tomorrow", and "10 Days". The unselected segments have no background; the selected segment is a pill-shaped container using `secondary-container` with `on-secondary-container` text.

### Cards
All weather data is encapsulated in cards. 
- **Main Forecast Card:** Large, using `display-lg` typography. 
- **Grid Cards:** Square-ish aspect ratio for metrics like "UV Index" and "Visibility".

### Bottom Navigation
A standard M3 Navigation Bar with 3-5 destinations. Active states use a tonal pill highlight around the icon. The background of the bar should be the `surface` color with 90% opacity and backdrop blur to maintain the glassmorphic theme.

### Weather Indicators
Dynamic elements like suns, clouds, and raindrops should use a soft 3D-effect (subtle inner glows) rather than flat icons, ensuring they feel "alive" within the glassmorphic containers.
